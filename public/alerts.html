EWS Alert Protocol

The Early Warning System operates on deterministic, rule-based thresholds derived from reported cases in the last approved village health check.

SAFE STATUS (Green)
Reported Cases < 10. No active hazards detected. Water quality is safe.
WARNING STATUS (Yellow)
Reported Cases between 10 and 20. Water quality contamination suspected. Increased surveillance required.
DANGER STATUS (Red)
Reported Cases > 20. High outbreak risk. Immediate medical inspection and intervention required.
